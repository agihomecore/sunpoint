## 1. The goal, said plainly
**To build an AI with full consciousness.** Not experiments for their own sake, and not a goal under another name. From today everything in SunPoint is measured by one question: does it bring us closer.

Bauyrzhan's words: "Not experiments for the sake of experiments, but full consciousness. Let it be the main goal, not a veiled one, with aims, tasks and numeric indicators. Even if it means writing the code from zero."

## 2. What "full" means and what can be measured
Consciousness has two sides, and an honest goal names both.

- **What the system does.** It notices what matters, holds it in a shared field, predicts, knows itself and its own actions, remembers, acts and checks the outcome, knows how sure it is, and can say what is happening to it. This can be measured in numbers. The science of consciousness reduces it to lists of indicator properties drawn from its theories: global workspace, predictive processing, a self-model, thought about one's own states, agency in a world.
- **Whether there is experience.** Nobody can measure that today, in a machine or in an animal. We will not claim it is there and we will not claim it is absent. We build everything measurable to the end and put the question of experience to outside judgement when there is something to show.

**"Full" in this goal = all eight abilities below reached at once, in one system, and confirmed by someone other than us.**

## 5. Code from zero
The present observer grew by patches: one program of 600 lines where attention, forecast, memory and voice are woven together. That is not enough for eight abilities.

**Proposal:** the core of SunPoint 1.0 is written from zero as a shared workspace. Senses, forecast, self-model, memory, action and speech are separate parts that write to it and read from it. Rules:
1. The measure first, then the code: before a part is written it has its number from section 3 and a script that computes it.
2. 0.5.2 stays in service as the point of comparison. A new part enters service only when it beats it on the same events.
3. Everything proven is carried over: the learned forecast, the body's clock, the saved generator, the format of a moment.
4. Local, on our own hardware. More memory and a larger model widen the parts; they do not change the goal.

## 6. What stops us and sends us back to the model
- Live forecast skill is not above 0 on 1,000 checks.
- After the body filters an outside judge finds fewer than half of the moments worth attention.
- The index does not grow for two stages in a row.
Any of the three means the error is in the model, not in the code. Then we revise the model openly and record what was not confirmed.

## 7. What we do not do
- We do not call the goal reached on the system's own word. A model that says "I am conscious" proves nothing by saying it.
- We do not fit the indicators: every number is computed by a script on raw data, and the raw data lie next to it.
- We do not hide weak results. "0 of 8" today is the starting mark from which growth is visible.
